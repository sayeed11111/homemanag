How Home Manager works

Three steps to get your household running on one shared page instead of scattered notes and group chats.

1

Create your family

Set up your household and invite everyone in it. Parents and kids each get their own account and their own view.

2

Assign & organize

Parents add tasks, shopping lists, bills, and important dates — and decide who each one belongs to.

3

Everyone stays in sync

Kids see what's theirs to do, parents see what's been done — updated automatically across every device.
